# HUL Calcutta Metro — Route Redesign: Key Benefits
**RS 218390 · 108 Salesmen · ~49,000 Active Outlets · Prepared by Stackbox · May 2026**

---

## What Changed
The existing sales routes for Calcutta Metro were redesigned from the ground up — same headcount, dramatically better coverage efficiency.

---

## 1. 73% Fewer Wasted Visits
| | Existing | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Instances of two salesmen at same outlet, same day | 8,171 / cycle | 2,192 / cycle |
| Outlets affected | 5,436 | 1,961 |

Each wasted visit is a salesman who travelled to an outlet that was already being covered by someone else that day — a call that could have gone to an uncovered outlet instead. The redesign gives each salesman distinct coverage for each day.

---

## 2. Territory Overlap: 22–52% → ~0%
| Product Category | Existing Overlap | Proposed Overlap |
|---|---|---|
| Detergents (DETS) | 37% | ~0% |
| Foods + Nutrition (FNB+NUTS) | 45% | ~0% |
| Personal Products (PP) | 52% | ~0% |

"Overlap" means two salesmen of the same product type both covering the same geography. One of them is redundant. The new design gives each salesman a unique, non-overlapping territory.

---

## 3. 100% Category-Specialist Salesmen
- **Before:** 53 of 107 salesmen (50%) carried mixed product portfolios across 2–3 categories
- **After:** 0 of 108 salesmen have mixed portfolios — every salesman specialises in exactly one category

A salesman handling detergents, foods, and personal products simultaneously has a third of the category depth of a specialist. Specialist focus = better product knowledge, higher outlet conversion, stronger relationships with category buyers.

---

## 4. 39% Fairer Workload
| | Existing | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Workload imbalance (CV) | 24.9% | 15.1% |

Coefficient of Variation (CV) measures spread in outlet load across salesmen. High CV = some salesmen are overloaded while others are under-used. The redesign brings loads closer together — more consistent performance, less burnout.

---

## 5. 27% Shorter Delivery Routes
| | Existing | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Average route length per beat | 4.4 km | 3.2 km |

Compact, non-overlapping territories mean salesmen travel less between outlets. Less travel time = more calls per day, lower fuel cost, and less fatigue. The 27% reduction translates directly to productivity and cost savings at scale (108 salesmen × 6 days/week).

---

## 6. 2,939 Duplicate Outlets Cleaned
2,939 ghost stores and duplicates were identified in the outlet master. Removing them saves coverage equivalent to **~13 salesmen's workload** — time currently spent calling on stores that don't exist or are double-counted.

---

## Bottom Line

| Metric | Existing | Proposed | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same-day duplicate visits | 8,171/cycle | 2,192/cycle | **−73%** |
| Territory overlap (avg) | 22–52% | ~0% | **Eliminated** |
| Mixed-category salesmen | 53/107 (50%) | 0/108 | **100% pure** |
| Workload imbalance | 24.9% | 15.1% | **−39%** |
| Avg delivery route length | 4.4 km | 3.2 km | **−27%** |
| Duplicate outlets in master | 2,939 | Cleaned | **Actionable** |

> This redesign does not add headcount. It improves the efficiency of the existing 108 salesmen by ensuring each visits the right outlets, on the right day, selling the right product.
